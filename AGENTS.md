# prior-ui

## Purpose
A study monorepo. The owner is a senior full-stack engineer working toward a
staff/architect role. It will hold several projects built with different
architectures (monolith, microservices, microfrontends, …) to practice and
compare them. Workspace layers are described in `README.md`.

The goal is to grow as an engineer, not to ship features. A feature is the
vehicle for learning, so optimize decisions for understanding and quality,
not speed.

## How to work
- Prefer industry standards and established patterns over the quickest
  solution. When there are several, show how major companies and systems
  approach it, compare the trade-offs, and recommend one.
- Think at staff level: API design, boundaries, scalability, maintainability,
  accessibility, and the cost of a decision for other teams and future changes.
- Build step by step in small sub-steps the owner can follow, and pause after
  each one for review before continuing.
- After each change or decision, add a **"What's worth learning here"** section:
  the pattern or principle behind it, why it was chosen over the alternatives,
  and any standards it relies on (WCAG, HTML spec, React docs, …).
